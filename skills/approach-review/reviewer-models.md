Choose from the first pool that has an available model: listed models, then other models with strong software-reasoning capability below the flagship tier, then flagships. Within that pool, prefer a family other than the author's or implementer's, use theirs only when the pool has no other, and take the most cost-effective model. The author or implementer records the reviewer's model, and whether it shares their family, alongside the review outcome (the finding dispositions, or the reviewer's no-findings reply), not in the reviewer's report.
