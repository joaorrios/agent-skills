# Small models

How much scaffolding a brief needs depends on how much judgment the recipient supplies on its own. A highly capable model infers convention, resolves ambiguity, and stops on its own. A small, fast model running classification, routing, or extraction has less room to do any of that, and terseness that reads as trust to a capable model reads as ambiguity to it.

For a small or fast recipient, move more of the task onto the narrow bridge:

- Spell out the steps in order.
- Spell out the output format, with varied examples when no schema can enforce it.
- State the decision criteria a more capable model would infer, such as how to break a tie or what counts as out of scope.
- Have it check its output against the format before answering.
