Add `full-auto`, a skill that takes a change end to end while the user is away and forks at the end: "full send" merges the PR, "full stop" hands it off ready for the user to merge. Both share proof for every change, review and PR tending with the skills available, a merge gate, pending blockers that hold back only the work depending on them, recorded product forks, and one round of questions at the end. Adapted from tstack's `full-send` and `full-stop`.
